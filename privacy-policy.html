Privacy Policy for RicoShot

Last updated: 29 May 2026

RicoShot (the “Game”) is developed and published by BruGames (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Game handles and how. We keep it deliberately simple because the Game is a small, single-player puzzle game and we collect as little as possible.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts, precise GPS location, or any other directly identifying personal information on our own servers. We do not have servers that store your personal data.

2. Information handled by third-party services

The Game uses a small number of Google services to show optional ads, process purchases, and optionally save your progress. These services may collect information as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows opt-in “rewarded” ads only — ads appear solely when you choose to watch one to unlock extra levels. There are no ads during gameplay. To serve these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process a device advertising identifier and related technical information (such as device type, IP address used to infer approximate region, and ad-interaction data) for the purposes of showing ads, measuring ad performance, and preventing fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland we show a consent request (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are used; you may choose non-personalized ads. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you choose to buy the one-time “Unlock all levels” product, the transaction is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details; Google handles payment and only tells the Game whether the purchase was completed.

Saved progress (Google Play Games — optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your level progress may be saved to your own Google account so it can sync across your devices. This data is stored by Google on your account, not by us. You can manage or delete Play Games saved data in your Google account settings.

On-device storage

The Game stores your settings and level progress locally on your device. This information stays on your device (or in your Google Play Games save, if signed in) and is removed when you uninstall the Game.

3. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured for a general audience.

4. Your choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you can change your ad-consent choice at any time within the Game’s consent options.
  • You can delete all on-device data by uninstalling the Game.
  • To request deletion of any data associated with you, contact us at the email below and we will help with what is within our control.

5. Data security

Information transmitted by the third-party services above is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
